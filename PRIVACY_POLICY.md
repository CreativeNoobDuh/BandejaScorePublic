# Bandeja Score Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 27, 2026

Bandeja Score is a tennis and padel scoring, recording, and highlight-creation app. The app is designed to work locally on your device and, when you choose, over your local Wi-Fi network between your own devices.

## Information We Collect

Bandeja Score does not collect, sell, or share personal information with the developer.

The app may create and store match-related files on your device, including recorded videos, score logs, scoreboard overlay data, rendered scoreboard videos, and highlight reels. These files remain on your device unless you choose to export, share, upload, or delete them.

## Camera and Microphone

Bandeja Score requests camera permission so you can record matches from inside the app.

Bandeja Score requests microphone permission because Android video recording can include audio with recorded videos. Audio and video recordings are stored locally on your device unless you choose to share or upload them.

## Local Network Use

Bandeja Score can connect two devices on the same local network so one device can control the score and another device can record video with a live scoreboard overlay. Match score updates and camera preview snapshots may be sent between your own devices over your local network during this feature.

These local network features do not send your data to the developer.

## Storage

Bandeja Score uses device storage so you can save recordings, rendered videos, highlight reels, logs, and debug exports. You can delete saved recordings and generated files from inside the app or from your device storage.

## Third-Party Services

Bandeja Score does not use third-party analytics or advertising services.

If you choose to upload or share a video using another service, such as YouTube, Google Drive, or a messaging app, that service's privacy policy applies to that upload or share action.

## Children

Bandeja Score is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes

This privacy policy may be updated as the app changes. Updates will be posted in this repository.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the Bandeja Score project page.
